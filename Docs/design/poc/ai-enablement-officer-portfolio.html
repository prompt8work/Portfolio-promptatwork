How a corporate engagement works

From your workflow to a trained team

Training starts with how your team works today, not with a fixed slide deck.

01

Discover

Workflow discovery session with team leads to find where AI will actually help.

02

Design

Tailor the curriculum to your roles, your tools and your real tasks.

03

Deliver

Live, hands-on sessions where people practise on their own work.

04

Equip

Hand over prompt libraries, templates, guides and usage guidelines.

05

Iterate

Review what the team is using, then refine the material and next session.

Feedback from step 05 loops back into step 02 for the next batch.
