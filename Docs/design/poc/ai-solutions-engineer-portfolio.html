Frequently asked questions

Which AI platforms do you work with?

Claude and the Anthropic API, Google Gemini, OpenAI and Groq, plus AI coding environments such as Claude Code, Cursor and Google Antigravity. I build on whichever platform your organisation has approved, and I can integrate any other model or tool you already use in your environment.

Can it connect to our internal systems?

Yes. Integrations run through REST APIs, the Google Drive API, OAuth 2.0 and MCP servers, so AI works with your data where it already lives.

How do you handle security?

Secure authentication, data handling and acceptable-use practices are part of the design, including builds for VPN-secured environments.

What do we get at hand-over?

Setup guides, usage guidelines and technical handover documentation for both end users and engineers.

Can you upskill our developers too?

Yes. I coach teams on Claude Code and AI-assisted development. See the training profile for workshops.

How is an engagement priced?

Every engagement is scoped to your team's needs, so pricing depends on what we build together. Tell me about your project and I'll share a tailored proposal.
