# Brand

The PromptAtWork lockup: the chevron-network logo tile beside the live wordmark, with "Prompt" in `text-primary` and "AtWork" in `brand-accent`.

- Markup: `<a class="paw-brand"><img class="paw-brand__logo" src="(Logos/promptatwork-mark.png)" alt=""><span>Prompt<span class="paw-brand__accent">AtWork</span></span></a>`. The image is decorative, because the wordmark carries the name.
- The tile is 36px with a 10px radius in the top nav, and 30px/8px in the dense TV header (`.paw-tvhead` resizes it).
- The mark alone is the favicon and the collapsed-sidebar logo. The full lockup image is for covers, slides and social posts only.
- Never recolour the tile or set the wordmark in another face. The navy tile stays as it is in light mode.
